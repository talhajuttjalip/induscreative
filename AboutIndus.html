About Us

We make video. We make sure it gets watched.

“

So we built a team that handles both under one roof, on purpose.

Indus Creative started from a fairly specific frustration: watching businesses hire a production company for a great-looking video, then a separate media buyer who had no context on why the video was built the way it was — and the two rarely talked. The video would get finished, then sit unoptimized for the platform it ended up running on.

We work with startups shooting their first product video, and with established brands running always-on paid video campaigns. Either way, the goal is the same: video that's built to do a specific job, not just look good in a portfolio.

What we're trying to do

Give businesses video content that's genuinely well made and genuinely built to perform — production and media strategy from the same team, working off the same brief.

Where we're headed

Building a reputation as the team businesses call when they want video handled end to end — not a production house that hands off to someone else once the file's rendered.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond to new inquiries?

Within one business day, usually faster. We'll set up a discovery call once we understand what you need.

How does a project actually start?

A consultation to understand your goals, audience, and budget; then a script and platform plan before anything gets produced.

What industries do you work with?

Startups through established brands, across SaaS, e-commerce, healthcare, real estate, hospitality, consumer goods, and professional services.

What do you actually offer?

Video production, animation, editing, and the paid media and SEO work that gets that video seen; as one connected service, not separate vendors.

Why work with Indus Creative instead of a production house or a media agency?

A production house hands you a file and moves on. A media agency runs ads on creative they didn't build and don't fully understand. We do both, which means the video is built with its distribution in mind from day one.

Ready to make video that actually does something?

Whether you need one strong brand film or an always-on video ad engine, let's figure out what's actually going to move your numbers.
